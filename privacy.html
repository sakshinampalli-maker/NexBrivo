Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • NexBrivo Solutions Private Limited

1. Overview

NexBrivo Solutions Private Limited ("NexBrivo", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of corporate clients, prospective partners, and individuals who visit our website or use our IT consultancy and cybersecurity services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver enterprise IT services, respond to inquiries, or evaluate career applications:

  • Contact Inquiries: Full name, corporate email address, business phone number, company name, and infrastructure requirements submitted via our consultation or quotation forms.
  • Careers Submissions: Candidate name, phone number, resume information, LinkedIn profile URL, and professional background.
  • Technical Logs: Standard server access logs (IP address, browser user-agent, operating system) collected automatically for network security and diagnostic purposes.

3. How We Use Collected Information

Information gathered through our site is used strictly for:

  • Preparing proposals, technical scopes, and Quotations for Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), cloud architecture, and cybersecurity audits.
  • Responding to emergency technical support or hotline requests.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements in Maharashtra and India.

4. Data Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

NexBrivo enforces strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA). We do not sell, rent, or trade client information or infrastructure telemetry to third parties. Data is accessible solely to authorized engineering and customer account personnel.

5. Information Security

We deploy enterprise-grade administrative, physical, and digital safeguards—including encrypted transport protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted access controls—to protect data submitted through this platform against unauthorized access or alteration.
